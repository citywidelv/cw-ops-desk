Form submissions

Every Jotform the team and our clients still fill out, in one place. Pick a form to review what came in. Client work requests also land on the Alerts card of the Ops Hub under the account's FSM.
Adding a form. In the Jotform builder open Settings, Integrations, Google Sheets, and add the Google Sheets integration on the City Wide account (send existing submissions too). Then add one row on the Feeds tab of the CW Jotform Feeds sheet: slug, label, group, account, form id, sheet id. Put workreq, workdone or review in the alert column when that form's submissions should also show on the Alerts card. No deploy needed.
