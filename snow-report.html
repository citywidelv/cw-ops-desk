Snow & Ice Service Report

Fill this out at the site, before you leave, for every snow visit. City Wide calculates your pay from this report: truck time while the truck is actually plowing or spreading, shovel labor per person while they are actually shoveling, and ice melt per bag used.

Read this first: how you get paid The truck is paid per hour only while it is actually plowing or spreading. A parked or idling truck is not paid. Shovel labor is paid per person, per hour, only while that person is actually shoveling. Anyone riding in or sitting in the truck is not paid. Never bill a person and the truck for the same block of time.
  • Example: three crew arrive in one truck. The truck plows 1 hour, then parks while two crew shovel 1 hour. Correct report: 1 truck hour, 2 shovel hours. Nothing else.
  • Overbilling the truck while it sits, crew while they ride, or ice melt you did not use will be docked from your pay. Repeat violations can end your agreement.
