Blank line between paragraphs. Start lines with - for a list. Put {button} on its own line where you want the red button; leave it out and the button goes at the end. Put {fsm} on its own line where you want their photo card. Fill-ins: {business} {contact} {market} {phone} {by} {fsm_name} {fsm_title} {fsm_email}. A whole line is left out if a fill-in on it comes back blank, so nothing ever reads "at the request of , ". Keep optional fill-ins on their own line.
