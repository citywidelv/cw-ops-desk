2Certificate holder

The holder is the customer's legal entity, letter for letter as they wrote it, at the address they gave. City Wide's entity is the named insured, never the holder.

Did the customer ask for more than one entity in the holder box?
The ACORD 25 holder box fits one name and address. Extra entities are normally listed as additional insureds in the description of operations. The broker decides; list them all.
