Work tickets to bill

Every maintenance work ticket the handyman or a vendor crew submits lands here under the FSM who owns the account. Each ticket gets billed once. Create the work order in CRM, then mark the ticket billed with your name on it. If a ticket is sitting under someone else and you did the work, reassign it to yourself first, then bill it. Whoever opens this page next sees who billed what, so nothing goes out to the client twice.

Billing tool

Use this tool to convert the work order from the handyman into entries that you can paste into the work order before you close it, so that the appropriate details are in the system for the client invoicing to avoid confusion.

If it is a return trip, a second or a third visit, and the handyman did not indicate that when they input it, you will have to add that detail when you copy and paste their notes.

You can also upload invoices into the tool. The invoices will be read to give you an input that you can use for any supplies that you are billing as consumables.
