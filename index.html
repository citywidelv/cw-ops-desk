What's new · September 25
The menus got a haircut. Everything is still here. It is just listed once.

171 menu rows became about 95. No page was removed or moved. If something you used is not where it was, it is in one of the places below, or one search away.

What changed at the top

  • Opportunities is its own menu now. Post, Active Postings, Vendor Responses, Live Board and Edit a Posting in one place.
  • Work Tickets moved under Accounts & Buildings, next to building sheets and account changes.
  • Vendor Profile is under Vendors. Everything about one vendor on one page.
  • Labels are short. A small chip on the right tells you where a link goesAdminVendor HubJotform

Where things moved

  • Vendor Resources (25 links) Vendors > Vendor Hub. Every page is a tile on the Vendor Hub home
  • Form Submissions list Team & Admin > Form Submissions. One page, every form, with counts
  • Active Vendors by type Vendors > Directory > your market. Pick Janitorial, Other Services or Prospects on the page
  • Google Sheet links Admin Hub > Ops Admin Desk. All 23 books in one list
  • Office Inventory actions Team & Admin > Office Inventory. Count, receive and issue are buttons on the page
  • Brady, Staples, HD Supply Admin Hub > Team & Admin > Ordering, and the Team Portal

Use the other hubs

  • Vendor Hub is for anything a vendor does. Invoices, uploads, the shop, guides, the wall. Send vendors there and go there to see what they see.
  • Admin Hub is the back office. Records one at a time, onboarding, background check results, uniform orders, every sheet.
  • Team Portal is the front door. Team apps, CRM, ordering and the Team Wall live there for every role.
  • Sales Hub has the calculators and the Building Survey for the sales team.

Fastest way to anything is the search box at the top. Type one word. Audit, exhibit, invite, COI, route. It searches every menu label and shows you the path, so you never have to open a dropdown to find a tool. On a computer, press / from anywhere on this page to jump to it.
