Night recaps

What the night managers filed. The ones that need you are first.
How this page counts

A recap needs a look when the score is under 8, a problem is still open, the client complained, a standard was not met, one of your questions came back No, the crew was out of uniform, or the night manager asked for you. Mark it handled once you have dealt with it.

Each night manager is expected to file 5 in-building recaps a night, every weekday that is not a holiday. Phone-only and photos-from-crew recaps are shown but do not count toward the 5. Routed means buildings an FSM sent on the Route Out. Tap a name to see routed stops with no recap.
