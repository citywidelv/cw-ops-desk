Office Inventory Count

Count what is on the shelf, record what moved, and this builds the Office Inventory block for the ACT. Every item needs a number before you can submit. If there are none on the shelf, put a 0, because a blank box means nobody looked. Vests and smocks are counted by size. Dispensers are counted two ways, what is in the office and what is already installed at accounts, so ordering decisions are made on real numbers.

1 Which office are you counting

The count, the summary email and the ACT block all follow this pick.
Counting after the first of the month Anything sold between the 1st and today has to be added back so the count reflects the month end position. Put the units sold for the calendar month in the Sold column from Business Central item movement for this company.

2 Chemicals

Stock we sell on. Last month carries over from the last count you submitted for this office. Expected is last month plus received minus sold and comped. Variance is your count against expected.

3 Spray bottles, labels and small parts

The silk screened bottles and the parts that go out with them.

4 Dispensers and install kits

EnvirOx gives us these at no charge, so the number that matters is where they are, not what they cost. In office is what is on the shelf right now. Installed at accounts is the running total hanging on walls. Put the building name in the note when one goes out.

5 Wall charts, literature and SDS

The paper packet that hangs next to every dispenser. All no charge from EnvirOx, counted so we know when to ask for more.

6 Apparel

Count every size. The size boxes add up to the count for the item, so the ACT still gets one number per item and we still know which sizes to reorder.

This is the Office Inventory block in ACT column order. Copy it straight into the bottom of the month sheet.
